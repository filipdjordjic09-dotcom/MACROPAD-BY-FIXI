# Journal

## 2026-11-09 - Project Design and Firmware Setup (4.5h)

I built a custom 4-key macropad with a rotary encoder from scratch for the Hack Club Hackpad project!

First, I designed a 2-part 3D enclosure in Onshape (85x60mm) featuring 4 switch cutouts, an encoder mounting hole, internal screw bosses, and a USB-C cutout for the microcontroller. Next, I created a custom schematic and PCB layout in KiCad 10, connecting 4 Cherry MX switches and an EC11 encoder directly to a Seeed Studio XIAO RP2040. Finally, I configured KMK Firmware in CircuitPython to control volume and shortcuts!
